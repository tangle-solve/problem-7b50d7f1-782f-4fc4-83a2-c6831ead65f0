### ALGO-000053: Does derandomizing PromiseZPP derandomize PromiseBPP?
- **Subfield:** Pseudorandomness, derandomization, and Boolean analysis
- **Open:** Prove or refute the implication \(\mathrm{PromiseZPP}\subseteq\mathrm P \Rightarrow \mathrm{PromiseBPP}\subseteq\mathrm P\).
- **Status:** Explicitly retained as an open problem in the 2024 Luca Trevisan memorial collection. Known relationships between zero-error and bounded-error randomness do not settle this implication for promise problems.
